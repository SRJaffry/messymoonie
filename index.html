About the club

Play, led entirely by your little one

Messy Moonie Play Club is a welcoming sensory play space where little ones explore at their own pace. Our sessions are designed to nurture curiosity, creativity, confidence, and connection through hands-on, baby-led play — with textures, colour, sound, and movement at the centre of everything we do.

  • There's no set outcome and no right or wrong way to play.
  • Babies and children move through activities at their own pace.
  • Caregivers are warmly encouraged to join in, observe, and enjoy it too.
  • Every session is built to support connection between child and grown-up.

Why sensory play

The benefits of sensory and baby-led play

Simple, gentle ways sensory play supports your child's early development — no exaggerated claims, just meaningful play.

01

Curiosity & exploration

Open-ended materials invite children to investigate freely.

02

Early brain development

Rich sensory input supports how young brains build connections.

03

Motor skills

Scooping, pouring, and squeezing build fine and gross motor strength.

04

Confidence & independence

Children learn to make their own choices about how they play.

05

Communication

Play sparks language, gesture, and social interaction with others.

06

Problem-solving

Open materials encourage early experimenting and figuring things out.

07

Learning through the senses

Touch, sound, sight, and movement all feed early learning.

08

Bonding time

Shared, unhurried play builds connection between child and caregiver.

09

Creative expression

Children explore ideas and feelings through open, playful materials.

Getting ready

What to bring

Messy play may involve water, paint, foam, food-based textures, sand, mud, or other sensory materials depending on the session theme — so come dressed for adventure.

A few friendly reminders below to help session day go smoothly for you and your little one.
Clothes that can get messy
A full change of clothes
A towel or wet wipes
Water or a drink
Socks or indoor shoes
Any comfort item your child needs
A willingness to explore and have fun
